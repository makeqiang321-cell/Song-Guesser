1. What guess song is

Guess song tests how little audio you need to name a record. Every round opens on a 0.1-second clip and only reveals more when you miss or skip, so the real score is how early you got it.

The format came from Heardle's name-the-intro idea, but modern rounds start at a tenth of a second and have no one-a-day limit.

2. How to play

Press play

The round opens on a 0.1-second clip.

Name the song

Type the title or artist and pick from the suggestions.

Get it right, round ends

Guessing on the shortest clip is the best result.

Miss or skip, clip grows

0.1s, 0.5s, 2s, 8s, then 15s.

Reroll

No daily lock. Play as many rounds as you want.

3. Reveal and difficulty

Two dials control how hard a round feels, and they do different jobs.

  • Reveal ladder: 0.1s, 0.5s, 2s, 8s, 15s. Same on every song. Guessing early is always better.
  • Difficulty: Easy to Impossible. It changes which songs you see, not how the clip is cut. Easy deals chart hits. Impossible deals album cuts.

Filters are the third dial. Lock a genre, decade, or artist and the pool shrinks to music you know. That is the fastest way to make Impossible winnable.

4. What you hear at 0.1 seconds

No melody, no lyric. Four things survive a tenth of a second:

  • Attack: the first milliseconds of a drum hit, pick, or synth stab.
  • Timbre: a snare's crack, a guitar's midrange, a synth's width.
  • Room and reverb: dry 2000s pop vs a big 80s snare.
  • Count-ins and producer tags: a breath, a stick click, a shouted name.

Headphones matter. Speakers strip the bass and low-mids where this texture lives.

5. How to get better

Listen for texture, not melody

Name the production sound, not the tune.

Build an era index

80s gated reverb. 90s grunge and acoustic intros. 2000s compressed pop. 2010s trap hats and EDM drops.

Drill one artist at a time

Hits first, then deep cuts, until intros are reflex.

Commit early or jump

If nothing surfaces, skip straight to 2–3 seconds. Nibbling pays a long clip for short-clip information.

Use headphones

Texture lives in the frequencies a speaker loses.

Replay before you type

Hear the slice again, and save your skips for your weak genres.

6. FAQ

What is guess song?
A music game where you name a real song from a very short clip. The less audio you need, the better.
How do you play guess song?
Press play, name the song or artist, and skip or miss to reveal a longer clip: 0.1s, 0.5s, 2s, 8s, 15s.
How can you guess a song in 0.1 seconds?
Read timbre and attack, not melody. Lock a genre, decade, or artist filter, then match the texture to a shortlist.
Is guess song free? Do I need an account?
Yes, and no. It runs in any browser, needs no signup or download, and has no one-a-day limit.
How many guesses do you get?
No fixed cap. Each miss or skip reveals a longer clip until 15 seconds shows the answer.
How do I get better at guess song?
Train on texture, build an era index, drill one artist at a time, and jump instead of nibbling.

Key takeaways

  • Guess song reads timbre and attack, not melody. The first 0.1s carry the fingerprint.
  • The reveal ladder is fixed. Difficulty changes which songs you see.
  • Filters shrink the pool and make Impossible winnable.
  • Train with an era index, one-artist drills, and "commit or jump."
