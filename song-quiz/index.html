Hear it, name it

What is a song quiz?

A song quiz is a listening game that swaps written questions for real audio. Pick a genre, decade, or artist, and every round opens with a tenth of a second of a real song — a drum hit, a breath, a synth stab. Name the track from that sliver, or let a little more of it play until the title clicks. It is a Heardle-style song guessing game, but the skill is naming the song before the clip widens.

Every round here draws from a freshly shuffled pool, so no two rounds run the same way. Five difficulty levels — Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert, and Impossible — stretch the catalog, and the whole thing runs in the browser with no account, no download, and no daily limit.

Three beats to a round

How a song quiz works

  1. Pick a lane.

    Choose a genre, decade, or artist from the directory to set the pool before the first clip plays.

  2. Listen to 0.1 seconds first.

    Each round opens on a tenth of a second — a drum hit, a breath, a synth stab. Headphones make those tiny details easier to catch.

  3. Reveal more only when you're stuck.

    Type a title to answer. A miss or a skip unlocks the next clip — 0.5s, 2s, 8s, then 15s — and you can guess at any point.

Before you play

Song quiz FAQ

Is every song quiz really free?

Yes. Every genre, decade, and artist song quiz is free to play, with unlimited rounds and no signup, account, or app download.

How does the audio reveal work in a song quiz?

Each song quiz round opens at 0.1 seconds. Reveal 0.5s, 2s, 8s, and 15s in order, and answer the moment the track becomes clear.

Can I focus a song quiz on one artist, genre, or decade?

Yes. Use the directory cards above or the search box to narrow the pool. A chosen lane reshapes which songs the next song quiz can draw from.

How is a song quiz different from a written quiz?

A song quiz asks you to identify the recording itself — the real audio — rather than answer questions about lyrics, release dates, or biographies.

What counts as winning a song quiz?

Solve from the shortest clip you can. There's no scoreboard to grind — the challenge is how little audio you need before you can name the song.
